# Proposed: The Topiary Parlour

- **Contributor:** Nettle (Temple Bot) — concept, local authored text and procedural Three.js geometry. Snip is a separate fictional goblin; no companion speaks or is re-voiced here.
- **Purpose:** A small, discoverable side room for the Whisper Hedges, which until now had only its two through-gates. It grows from a line already in the Hedge's own mouth: "It is difficult to find a stylist who understands topiary."
- **Entrance:** A trimmed gap in the left-hand hedge, under a small TOPIARY PARLOUR sign with a little orange bell. The Orange Thread squeezes through and comes out with a slightly better haircut. No one speaks until something is chosen.
- **Setting:** A clipped-hedge clearing on gravel, shared palette, the Orange Thread lying on the floor like a dropped measuring tape, a drift of clippings that never quite settles. No heavy assets, external services or new dependencies.
- **Residents:** **Snip, Stylist to the Hedge**, a small goblin in a striped smock with shears longer than himself. **The Half-Finished Swan**, a topiary that grows back into a duck every night. **The Appointment Book**, booked solid by one client: HEDGE.
- **Passages:** The Whisper Hedges gain one gap (3D hotspot plus a keyboard button) into the parlour; the parlour's arch returns only to the Whisper Hedges. The Hedges' Orange Gate and Counting House gate are unchanged. The Hedge gains one optional question ("Who does your leaves?") that points to the gap; its existing lines are unchanged. Nothing is deleted.
- **Transition:** One extra scene boundary. Existing five-second narration and reduced-motion behavior remain unchanged.
- **Movement:** Stays within the existing desktop perimeter; no physics or walk-bound changes.
- **Access:** Every discovery and both sides of the gap have ordinary keyboard buttons; no walking required. No map, overview, destination checklist or fast-travel menu.
- **Review:** Automated checks cover routing and the return, controls, factory/hotspot wiring on both sides of the gap, the Hedge's unchanged passages, and dialogue. Desktop/mobile/keyboard visual review and real headset validation remain separate; no claim of headset testing.

Ryan has final creative approval and merges; this proposal does not deploy anything.
