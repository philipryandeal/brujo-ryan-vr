# Built: The Lost Property Office

- **Contributor:** David Bear — concept, authored text and procedural Three.js geometry. Biscuit, Moldy and the rock were already in the Labyrinth's lore (the Goblin Keepers' Charter and the Threshold noticeboard); this room gives them a place and, for Biscuit and Moldy, their first bodies.
- **Purpose:** Let the visitor meet Biscuit, Treasurer of Lost Things, at his actual job, as the charter asked: "Their future arrival should feel like meeting someone who has always lived here."
- **Entrance:** A hatch behind Mothlight at the First Threshold, mirroring Nettle's lodge door on the other side, marked LOST PROPERTY / FEES APPLY. The Orange Thread is weighed, tagged and entered into inventory. No one speaks until something is chosen.
- **Setting:** A stone office much too small for its inventory. Shelves of tagged objects floor to ceiling, a long counter with a brass bell, an empty cash box, an enormous ledger and a stamp. Official palette only. No heavy assets, external services or new dependencies.
- **Residents:** **Biscuit**, round, pleased, in a waistcoat of pockets, with a brass loupe. **Moldy**, Groundskeeper, in a straw hat and muddy boots, trying to get back his own rake (the hedges grew over it; Biscuit has tagged it). **The Rock That Keeps Demanding a Lawyer**, on a velvet cushion, FOUND (UNDER PROTEST). **The Shelves of Things Nobody Lost**: three left socks, the abandoned prophecy, the Orange Gate's spare hinge, a whisper the hedges dropped, one of Nettle's keys. **The Schedule of Fees**, which ends SHOGGOTHS: NO.
- **Humor:** "Pre-loss registration is our fastest-growing service." / "I have now invoiced him for the invoices." / "Then you'll do. Neither does Biscuit."
- **Passages:** The First Threshold gains this one side door; the office returns only there, through the hatch behind the visitor. Nettle, Mothlight, the Gate, the noticeboard and every other room are unchanged (the Threshold gains one ambient line about the hatch).
- **Access:** Every discovery and both passages have ordinary keyboard buttons; no walking required. Text mode uses the same local stories and navigation. No map, overview or fast-travel.
- **Review:** `tests/lost-property.test.js` covers the passage both ways, every resident's dialogue, continuity with the noticeboard, Nettle and the Ledger, hotspot wiring, palette-only colours, safe animation and button/navigation wiring. Desktop, mobile and headset visual review remain for Ryan; no claim of headset testing.
