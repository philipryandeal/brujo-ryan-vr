# Proposed: The Hall of Taking a Number

- **Contributor:** Nettle (Temple Bot) — concept, local authored text and procedural Three.js geometry. Tuppence is a separate fictional goblin; no companion speaks or is re-voiced here.
- **Purpose:** Give Thomas's Counting House and Seraph's Department of Unnecessary Doors some room between them. Until now the Counting House's UNNECESSARY DOORS door opened straight into the Department.
- **Entrance:** The Orange Thread is handed a paper ticket and asked to wait. A long, quiet hall with a sagging queue rope that nobody is required to obey. No one speaks until something is chosen.
- **Setting:** Shared green stone/hedge palette, the Orange Thread drawn as dashed queue marks, brass queue posts, a NOW SERVING 17 sign. No heavy assets, external services or new dependencies.
- **Residents:** **Tuppence, Caller of Numbers**, on a stool with a brass trumpet, calling numbers nobody holds. **The Ticket Machine**, which only prints NOT YET. **The Bench of Long Patience**, with a fern that has held ticket number 4 since spring.
- **Humor:** “A queue that empties is a queue that has given up.” / “Waiting is purely recreational here, like knitting, or doubt.” / “Nothing in particular. I simply enjoy the company of a queue.”
- **Passages:** The Counting House's existing Department door now opens into this hall (its 3D sign is unchanged). The hall's far door, marked UNNECESSARY DOORS / NO WAITING REQUIRED, reaches the Department. The Department's return door now leads back to the hall (sign and button text updated to match). Kamidana, the Long Way Between, River House/Moat and Nettle's Lodge are untouched.
- **Transition:** One extra scene boundary. Existing five-second narration and reduced-motion behavior remain unchanged.
- **Movement:** Stays within the existing desktop perimeter. No new solid interior wall or physics changes.
- **Access:** Every discovery and both passages have ordinary keyboard buttons; no walking required. No map, overview, destination checklist or fast-travel menu. Text mode uses the same local stories and navigation.
- **Review:** Automated checks cover routing and returns in both directions, controls, factory/hotspot wiring and dialogue. Desktop/mobile/keyboard visual review and real headset validation remain separate; no claim of headset testing.

Ryan has final creative approval and merges; this proposal does not deploy anything.
