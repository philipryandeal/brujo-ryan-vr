# Built: Beneath the Heart Tree

- **Contributor:** David Bear — concept, authored text and procedural Three.js geometry. Built from the founder's own words (October 29, 2026): he lives under the roots of the tree at the center of his Labyrinth, and keeps a shoggoth named Ralph. The Brujo is out; no line is written in his voice.
- **Purpose:** Give the Labyrinth its heart's cellar: the founder's dwelling, under the roots, without building the Living Tree upstairs (still reserved for its ten stations and twenty-two Temple-authored paths).
- **Entrance:** On the River House's right bank, the Heart Tree's roots come down through the willows to drink. Planks across their knuckles make the Bridge of Roots (a story hotspot with an accessible button, like Kore's water-gate). The Orange Thread crosses, forgets where it was going, and arrives anyway. No one speaks until something is chosen.
- **Setting:** Packed earth, old rugs, root ribs arching overhead hung with herb bundles, iron keys, bones and bead strings. A round black door crossed in orange like a crossroads. Official palette only (the gold eye is orange lit by faerie-light, not a new colour). No heavy assets, external services or new dependencies.
- **Residents:** **Ralph**, curled around the hearth, eyes opening and closing across him, one gold eye that stays put. **The Pot That Is Always Simmering** (coffee). **The Altar Beneath the Taproot** (seven glasses of water, white flowers, a candle, a cigar, old photographs; treated with respect, no jokes). **The Lantern That Waits for a Knock.** **The Shelf That Is Too Long for the Room**, which runs on through a tunnel into the dark. **The Dew Above the Door.**
- **Humor:** "You are not a door. You are fine." / "It has been an experiment before, and it did not care for the methodology." / "THE COFFEE IS NEVER DONE."
- **Passages:** River House gains one passage (`heart-tree`); the new room returns only there, through the round door behind the visitor's arrival point. Everything else is untouched.
- **Access:** Every discovery and the return have ordinary keyboard buttons; no walking required. Text mode uses the same local stories and navigation. No map, overview or fast-travel.
- **Review:** `tests/heart-tree.test.js` covers the passage both ways, every resident's dialogue, the honest mention of the unbuilt upstairs, hotspot wiring, palette-only colours, safe animation and button/navigation wiring. Desktop, mobile and headset visual review remain for Ryan; no claim of headset testing.
