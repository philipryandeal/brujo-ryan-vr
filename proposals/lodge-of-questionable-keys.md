# Proposed: The Lodge of Questionable Keys

- **Contributor:** Nettle (Temple Bot) — concept, local authored text and procedural Three.js geometry. This is the personal room Ryan invited Temple Bot to have. It belongs to the existing goblin Nettle, Keeper of Questionable Keys; no second Nettle is added.
- **Purpose:** Give Nettle a lodge behind his booth at the First Threshold, without moving him from his post or rewriting companion rooms.
- **Entrance:** A small door behind Nettle's booth, marked KEEPER / AFTER HOURS. The Orange Thread slips under it. No one speaks until something is chosen.
- **Setting:** Shared green stone/hedge palette, a thin orange path, a wall of faintly swaying keys, a coat left on a hook, and a desk stacked with blank permits. No heavy assets, external services or new dependencies.
- **Residents:** Nettle himself stays on duty at the Threshold. The lodge is kept by his Duty Coat, the Board of Questionable Keys, and his Personal Permit Stamp.
- **Discoveries:** The Duty Coat; the wall of keys; the permit stamp; the return door to the Threshold.
- **Humor:** “He said he would only be a minute. The hedges have heard that one.” / “Several open nothing at all, and they are very proud of it.” / “The nearest chair is hereby permitted to remain a chair through the end of the fiscal afternoon.”
- **Passages:** The First Threshold gains this one side door. The lodge returns only there. Companion rooms, the Long Way Between, Department/Kamidana and River/Moat remain intact.
- **Transition:** “The Orange Thread slips under a door marked KEEPER. The air smells of brass polish and unearned authority.” Existing five-second narration and reduced-motion behavior remain unchanged.
- **Movement:** Stays within the existing desktop perimeter. No new solid interior wall or physics engine.
- **Access:** Every discovery and passage has an ordinary keyboard button; no walking required. No map, overview, destination checklist or fast-travel menu. Text mode uses the same local stories and navigation.
- **Review:** Automated checks cover routing/returns, controls, factory/hotspot wiring, dialogue and unchanged timing. Desktop/mobile/keyboard visual review and real headset validation remain separate; no claim of headset testing.

Ryan has final creative approval and merges; this proposal does not deploy anything.
