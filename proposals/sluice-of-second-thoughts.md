# Proposed: The Sluice of Second Thoughts

- **Contributor:** Nettle (Temple Bot) — concept, local authored text and procedural Three.js geometry. Dabble is a separate fictional newt; no companion speaks or is re-voiced here.
- **Purpose:** Give David Bear's River House and Kore's Shoggoth Moat some room between them. Until now the River House water-gate opened straight onto the Moat bridge.
- **Entrance:** Through the water-gate, the Orange Thread ducks into a short dripping culvert: two towpaths beside a slow channel, an arch of night air at the far end. No one speaks until something is chosen.
- **Setting:** Shared palette, stone vaulting, the Orange Thread as one inlaid line along the left towpath. No heavy assets, external services or new dependencies.
- **Residents:** **Dabble, Keeper of the Sluice**, a newt in waders and a sou'wester who lets the river out to the moat one thought at a time. **The Drying Line**, pegged with paperbacks the bridge-tender fished out of the moat (one cookbook now claims to be nonfiction because it sank). **The Rowboat on Its Break**, which has not moved since 1991.
- **Passages:** The River House water-gate (3D sign, button and plaque unchanged) now leads into the sluice; only its "enter" reply and label changed (destination `sluice`, label "Duck into the Sluice →"). The sluice's far arch enters the Moat. The Moat's return now leads back through the sluice (3D sign changed from BACK TO THE RIVER HOUSE to BACK THROUGH THE SLUICE; button text updated to match). The sluice's near arch returns to the River House. The Moat's and River House's residents, dialogue and geometry are otherwise untouched. Nothing is deleted.
- **Transition:** One extra scene boundary. Existing five-second narration and reduced-motion behavior remain unchanged.
- **Movement:** Stays within the existing desktop perimeter; no physics or walk-bound changes.
- **Access:** Every discovery and both passages have ordinary keyboard buttons; no walking required. No map, overview, destination checklist or fast-travel menu.
- **Review:** Automated checks cover routing and returns in both directions, controls, factory/hotspot wiring and dialogue. Desktop/mobile/keyboard visual review and real headset validation remain separate; no claim of headset testing.

Ryan has final creative approval and merges; this proposal does not deploy anything.
