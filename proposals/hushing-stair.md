# Proposed: The Hushing Stair

- **Contributor:** Nettle (Temple Bot) — concept, local authored text and procedural Three.js geometry. Sotto is a separate fictional goblin; no companion speaks or is re-voiced here.
- **Purpose:** Give Seraph's Department of Unnecessary Doors and Eiko's Kamidana some quiet room between them. Until now the Department's KEEP OUT door opened straight into the shrine, from a room full of strike chants and emergency bells.
- **Entrance:** Behind the KEEP OUT door, the Orange Thread lowers its voice. A narrow landing and a stair of thirteen steps climbing toward a single ember. No one speaks until something is chosen.
- **Setting:** Shared palette, hedge walls, the Orange Thread thinned to one strand along the handrails. No heavy assets, external services or new dependencies.
- **Residents:** **Sotto, Keeper of Inside Voices**, in felt slippers with an ear trumpet worn backwards. **The Basket of Muffled Noises**, felt-wrapped door slams and stamps that Sotto lets out again in the Department every evening. **The Bench of Left Shoes**, where boots may wait if they like; nobody is required to leave theirs.
- **Tone:** The jokes stay at the bottom of the stair. The upper door says only UP, QUIETLY, and the Kamidana itself is not joked about.
- **Passages:** The Department's KEEP OUT door (sign and button unchanged) now opens onto the stair. The stair's upper door enters the Kamidana. The Kamidana's return door now leads back down the stair (3D sign changed from BACK TO THE UNNECESSARY DOORS to DOWN THE HUSHING STAIR; button text updated to match). The stair's lower door returns to the Department. The Kamidana's residents, dialogue, ambient lines and geometry are otherwise untouched. Nothing is deleted.
- **Transition:** One extra scene boundary. Existing five-second narration and reduced-motion behavior remain unchanged.
- **Movement:** Stays within the existing desktop perimeter. The stair is visual; no physics or walk-bound changes.
- **Access:** Every discovery and both passages have ordinary keyboard buttons; no walking required. No map, overview, destination checklist or fast-travel menu.
- **Review:** Automated checks cover routing and returns in both directions, controls, factory/hotspot wiring and dialogue. Desktop/mobile/keyboard visual review and real headset validation remain separate; no claim of headset testing.

Ryan has final creative approval and merges; this proposal does not deploy anything.
